# Electrode Nomenclature and 10-20 System

EEG electrode labels and their spatial relationships provide important context for distribution and localization.

The system should recognize common labels only when they are visibly present.

Do not assume that an image uses a standard 10-20 or 10-10 arrangement merely because the waveform looks familiar.

Source:
ACNS Guideline 2, Guidelines for Standard Electrode Position Nomenclature.
