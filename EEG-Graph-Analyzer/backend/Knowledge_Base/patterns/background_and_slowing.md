# Background and Slowing

## Background

The background should be described before focusing on isolated abnormalities.

Relevant observations include:
- dominant activity
- organization
- symmetry
- continuity
- variability
- state

## Slowing

Slowing can be focal or generalized. Its significance depends on age, state, clinical context, and persistence.

The image agent should say what is visible, for example:
"Prominent slow activity is present in the displayed channels."

It should not automatically convert slowing into a specific disease.

Source:
American Epilepsy Society EEG atlas, NCBI Bookshelf, 2016.
