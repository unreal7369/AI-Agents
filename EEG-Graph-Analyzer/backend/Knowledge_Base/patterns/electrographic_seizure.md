# Electrographic Seizure — High-Caution Pattern

ACNS 2021 provides standardized terminology and criteria for electrographic seizures.

For an image-only agent:
- do not declare a seizure from a sharp transient alone;
- do not declare a seizure from rhythmicity alone;
- do not claim evolution unless the displayed data actually show temporal change;
- do not use unseen clinical information;
- do not provide treatment recommendations.

When evidence is incomplete, use:
"Possible ictal-pattern features are present, but the supplied image is insufficient for definitive classification."

Source:
Hirsch LJ et al., ACNS Standardized Critical Care EEG Terminology: 2021 Version.
