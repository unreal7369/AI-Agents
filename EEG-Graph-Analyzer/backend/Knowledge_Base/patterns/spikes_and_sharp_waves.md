# Spikes and Sharp Waves

Sharp transients and spikes are waveform descriptors, not diagnoses.

When evaluating a possible epileptiform discharge, consider:
- morphology
- field/distribution
- polarity
- background relationship
- repetition
- state
- artifact
- benign variants

A sharp-contoured waveform in one screenshot is not automatically an interictal epileptiform discharge.

Source:
American Epilepsy Society EEG atlas, NCBI Bookshelf, 2016.
