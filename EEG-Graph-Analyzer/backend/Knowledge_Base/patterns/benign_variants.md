# Benign EEG Variants and Mimics

Benign variants can resemble epileptiform activity.

Examples described in the AES EEG atlas include:
- wicket waves
- rhythmic mid-temporal theta of drowsiness
- benign small sharp spikes
- 14- and 6-Hz positive bursts
- 6-Hz phantom spike-and-wave
- subclinical rhythmic electrographic discharge of adults (SREDA)
- midline theta

These should be considered when morphology and context support them.

Do not label a benign variant solely from a name match. The actual waveform appearance, distribution, state, and context matter.

Source:
American Epilepsy Society EEG atlas, NCBI Bookshelf, 2016.
