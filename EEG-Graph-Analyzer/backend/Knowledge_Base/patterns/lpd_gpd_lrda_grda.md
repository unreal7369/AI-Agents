# LPD, GPD, LRDA, and GRDA

## LPD
Lateralized periodic discharges.

## GPD
Generalized periodic discharges.

## LRDA
Lateralized rhythmic delta activity.

## GRDA
Generalized rhythmic delta activity.

These are ACNS standardized terminology categories, not diagnoses by themselves.

The image system should verify that morphology, distribution, recurrence, and frequency information are sufficiently visible before applying one of these labels.

If the montage or channel coverage is incomplete, use cautious descriptive language.

Source:
Hirsch LJ et al., ACNS Standardized Critical Care EEG Terminology: 2021 Version.
