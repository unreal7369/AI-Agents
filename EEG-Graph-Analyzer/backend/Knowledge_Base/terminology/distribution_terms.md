# EEG Distribution Terms

Common distribution concepts include:
- generalized
- lateralized
- bilateral independent
- multifocal
- focal/localized

Use the term only when the visible channels provide enough spatial information.

"Generalized" should not be used simply because several displayed channels look similar if the image is cropped or channel coverage is incomplete.

"Lateralized" requires adequate evidence of asymmetry/distribution across the displayed channels.

Source:
ACNS standardized EEG terminology and montage guidance.
