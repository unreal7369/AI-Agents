# ILAE 2025 Seizure Classification

The ILAE updated its operational classification of epileptic seizures in 2025.

The main classes are:
- focal
- generalized
- unknown whether focal or generalized
- unclassified

The classification provides standardized seizure terminology.

## RAG rule

ILAE seizure classification should not be used to turn a visually suspected EEG waveform into an epilepsy diagnosis.

The image agent should first describe the EEG finding, then use clinical terminology cautiously, and clearly separate EEG evidence from the clinical diagnosis.

Source:
Beniczky S, Trinka E, Wirrell E, et al. Updated classification of epileptic seizures: Position paper of the International League Against Epilepsy. Epilepsia. 2025.
