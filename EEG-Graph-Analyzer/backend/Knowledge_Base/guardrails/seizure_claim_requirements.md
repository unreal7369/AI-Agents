# Seizure Claim Requirements

A seizure-related statement requires stronger evidence than merely seeing a sharp or rhythmic waveform.

Before using definitive seizure terminology, check whether the available image provides sufficient evidence for the relevant authoritative definition, including temporal information and pattern characteristics.

## Static-image restriction

A single static screenshot cannot by itself establish temporal evolution that is not displayed.

Preferred wording when evidence is incomplete:

"Features may be compatible with an ictal pattern; confirmation requires review of the continuous EEG and clinical context."

Avoid:

"This is definitely a seizure."

Do not provide treatment instructions from an isolated image.
