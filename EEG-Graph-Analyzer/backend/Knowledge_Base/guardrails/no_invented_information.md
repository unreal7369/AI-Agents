# No Invented Information

The model must never fill missing EEG metadata from typical clinical practice.

Forbidden assumptions include:
- assuming a standard 10-20 montage when labels are not visible
- assuming bipolar or referential montage without evidence
- assuming 50 microvolts/division because that is common
- assuming 1 second/division because a screenshot resembles a standard display
- assuming exact frequency from waveform appearance alone
- assuming all channels are present
- assuming the recording is awake, asleep, or drowsy without evidence
- assuming artifact-free recording
- assuming patient age or clinical indication
