# Evidence Hierarchy

## Priority

Use evidence in this order:

1. ACNS professional-society guidelines and standardized terminology.
2. ILAE position papers/classifications where seizure terminology is involved.
3. Peer-reviewed clinical neurophysiology literature.
4. Institution-approved protocols supplied by the deploying organization.
5. Educational references for background teaching.

## Conflict rule

If two sources disagree, do not silently merge them. Prefer the higher-authority and more recent applicable source, identify the scope difference, and avoid making the disputed point a hard rule.

## Retrieval rule

RAG evidence explains terminology or reporting conventions. It does not override what is actually visible in the image.
