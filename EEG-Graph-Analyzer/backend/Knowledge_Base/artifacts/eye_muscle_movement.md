# Eye, Muscle, and Movement Artifact

## Eye/blink
Often prominent in frontal/frontopolar channels and may create large slow or sharp-looking deflections.

## Muscle/EMG
Often appears as low-amplitude, high-frequency activity and can obscure or mimic cerebral fast activity.

## Movement
May create abrupt irregular changes that resemble cerebral abnormalities.

Use "possible artifact" when the image does not permit confident classification.

Source:
American Epilepsy Society EEG atlas, NCBI Bookshelf, 2016.
