# Electrode, Cardiac, and Electrical Artifact

Potential mimics include:
- electrode pop/contact artifact
- pulse/cardiac artifact
- electrical interference

A periodic-looking waveform may reflect a physiological or technical source rather than cerebral activity.

The image agent should look for distribution and morphology consistent with artifact, but should not make a definitive artifact diagnosis from limited evidence.

Source:
American Epilepsy Society EEG atlas, Appendix 4, NCBI Bookshelf, 2016.
