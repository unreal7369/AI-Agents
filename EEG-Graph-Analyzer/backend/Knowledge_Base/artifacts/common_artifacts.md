# Common EEG Artifacts

Common physiological and nonphysiological artifacts include:
- eye movement/blink
- muscle/EMG
- movement
- cardiac/pulse
- respiratory
- sweat
- glossokinetic/tongue movement
- electrode/contact artifact
- electrode pop
- electrical/environmental interference

Artifacts may mimic abnormal cerebral activity.

Source:
American Epilepsy Society EEG atlas, Appendix 4: Common Artifacts During EEG Recording, NCBI Bookshelf, 2016.
