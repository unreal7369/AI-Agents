# EEG Findings Wording

Prefer descriptive language:

- "The displayed background appears..."
- "Prominent slow activity is visible..."
- "Sharp-contoured transients are present..."
- "The pattern appears more prominent in the displayed right-sided channels..."

Avoid unsupported certainty:

- "The patient has epilepsy."
- "This proves a seizure."
- "The lesion is in the temporal lobe."
- "The recording is definitely normal."

The report should distinguish visible findings from clinical interpretation.
